What a video file carries

  • Where it was filmed: phones store GPS coordinates in the file's metadata, the same way they do for photos.
  • A GPS track: action cameras, dashcams and drones often record location, speed and sometimes motion data as a separate track that runs alongside the video, second by second.
  • The device: make, model and the software version that recorded or edited it.
  • When: the creation date and time.

Audio files carry tags too: names, the software that made them, and sometimes notes or comments added along the way.

How the clean copy is made

The video and audio are copied into a new file without re-encoding, so there's no loss of quality and it's quick even for long clips. Only the video, audio and subtitle streams are kept, which drops any GPS or motion track. All metadata is wiped, and the clean copy is scanned again before you download it.

What it can't remove

Metadata removal doesn't change what's on screen. Street signs, house numbers, landmarks, a reflection in a window, or someone saying a name out loud all survive. Watch the clip once with that in mind before you post it.

Supported formats

MP4, MOV, M4A, MKV, WebM, MP3, FLAC and WAV.
