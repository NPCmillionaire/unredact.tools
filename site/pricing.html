How tokens work

You get a token, not an account.

After you pay, we show you a random code once. It holds your credits, and that's all we know about it. No email, no password, no profile. Your browser remembers it; save a copy somewhere safe.

We store only a hash of the token and its balance. We never link a token to the files you scan.

Example token

UR-7F3K-9QXM-2WLD-H8PA

We can't recover a lost token. That's the point.

Questions

I lost my token. Can you restore it?

No. We don't know who you are, so there's nothing to restore it from. Save the file we offer after checkout.

Does the AI see my files?

Only when you press an AI button, only that file, with metadata stripped first. The privacy page will list exactly which model and provider.

Is the free version crippled?

No. Every detector and the full sanitizer are free. Credits pay for AI compute and bulk processing.

Can I run it myself?

Yes. It's open source: pip install, or one Docker command. Teams can license the hosted extras for self-hosting.
