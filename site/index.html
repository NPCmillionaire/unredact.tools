What we catch

The obvious metadata, and the things most tools miss.

GPS, altitude, heading

Down to the floor of the building and the direction you faced.

Device serial numbers

Camera body and lens serials tie every photo to one phone.

Failed redactions

Black boxes drawn over text that is still selectable underneath.

Earlier PDF versions

Incremental saves keep every previous draft inside the file.

Tracked changes & comments

Deleted text, reviewer names, and Windows usernames in paths.

Printer tracking dots

Yellow dot patterns that encode a printer's serial and print time.

Faces, plates, QR codes

Readable boarding passes, license plates and sensitive text in pixels.

Camera sensor fingerprint

Sensor noise links an "anonymous" photo to your other photos.
